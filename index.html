About

// summary

I build backend systems for a cybersecurity product used by thousands of analysts — mostly microservices, event pipelines, and internal tooling that has to keep working when traffic spikes or a queue backs up, not just on a good day.

A lot of my time goes into the unglamorous half of reliability: schema and query tuning, caching layers, autoscaling policies, and dashboards that catch a problem before it becomes an incident. I'd rather ship something boring that holds up than something clever that doesn't.

Outside of core backend work, I've been folding LLM-based tooling into internal workflows — things like natural-language querying over structured data — as a way to remove repetitive manual steps for the teams I support.
